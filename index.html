About Neha

I know what it costs to start over.

"Everyone tells you the destination. Nobody gives you the roadmap."

And for immigrant women, it's worse than that. We're not even given an accurate map of the terrain — the barriers, the invisible rules, the tax on starting over that nobody names until you're already paying it.

That's why I built this.

I've spent two decades at the highest levels of global tech and finance — leading multi-million dollar transformations across four continents. And still, nothing prepared me for rebuilding my professional identity from scratch.

Resilience is a muscle — but even strong muscles get tired.

Sawubona Trail exists because belonging is not automatic. It's a choice we have to make every single morning — and it's easier when we make it together. Sawubona is a Zulu word meaning "I see you, and by seeing you, I bring you into existence."

You don't need to choose between being successful and being whole. You just need someone who's been on the trail before you.
